🧹 I Am a Classroom Helper!

Taking care of ourselves, our toys, and our friends!

📢 Teacher: read everything out loud. Let the kids tap — tapping is the lesson. Every word the children need is spoken when they tap.

🟢🔴 Non-verbal / emerging communicators: use dual-sided visual paddles — Green Smiley = Helper, Red Circle = Oops — so children can answer the game without speaking.
