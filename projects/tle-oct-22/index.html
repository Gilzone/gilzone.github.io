2 · Mini-Lesson: Squish the Bag!

First we read Breathe Like a Bear — one big leaf breath together. Then the pumpkin investigation begins!

Seeds! Pulp! Skin! What's the stem for? Scoop the pulp into a sealed bag — now squish it! What does it feel like? Slimy? Bumpy?

⚠️ Teacher only cuts the pumpkin. Children squish the sealed bag — no sharp tools for little hands.
