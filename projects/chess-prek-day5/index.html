Day 5: Save the King! 🚨

The most important rule in all of chess.

📢 Teacher: when an enemy points at the king, shout “CHECK!” together. Then help the king run somewhere safe.

Part 1 — What is “check”?

CHECK means an enemy piece is pointing at your king — it could gobble him next! You must move the king somewhere safe.

The red rook is pointing at the king. Tap the king, then tap a green safe square.

Part 2 — Game: escape!

The king is in check! Find a safe square — 5 escapes to win.

Bonus — What is checkmate?

If the king is in check and has nowhere safe to run — that’s CHECKMATE. Game over!
