Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/19/26
Central Focus:

Students will practice table manners and meet letters A–G with their TLE characters.

Lesson # 12 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will read the Penny Polite reader together.
  • Students will practice please, thank you, you’re welcome, and chewing with mouth closed.
  • Students will learn letters A–G, each with a motion and sound.
Resources and Materials:

Penny Polite reader, letter cards A–G, pretend snack props

Learning Objectives:

I can eat politely: please, thank you, mouth closed. I know letters A to G and their sounds.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Relationship Skills):

Children use polite words and actions with others, and take turns in shared activities.

TimeActivity:
7 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and Manners Matter — Penny Polite reader (begin).
Teacher: Opens the reader: “Penny eats with her tummy to the table. Can you show me?”
Students: Show tummy-to-the-table posture.
6 min Mini-lesson: Magic words.
Teacher: Models with pretend crackers and cups: “Please… thank you… you’re welcome” — chewing with mouth closed.
Students: Practice the three magic words with a partner.
14 min Activity: Read together + manners pretend snack + letter cards A–G.
Teacher: Reads Penny Polite with the class; sets out the pretend snack; then holds up letter cards: Alex Alligator (chomp arms!) · Billy Badger · Carrie Canary · Danny Dog · Edgar Elk · Felicity Fish · Gertie Goat.
Students: Practice the snack manners — gently corrected with humor. Then: each letter gets a motion + sound.
4 min Game: Which character?
Teacher: Does a letter’s motion WITHOUT naming it.
Students: Shout the character, the letter, and the sound. First correct voice wins the round.
4 min Closure: “Show me polite eating — tummy to the table, please!”
Students: Everyone demonstrates; the class gives a polite round of applause.
Assessment:
  • Observation during the pretend snack: does the child use please and thank you, and chew with mouth closed?
  • Exit check: show me polite eating — tummy to the table, please!
