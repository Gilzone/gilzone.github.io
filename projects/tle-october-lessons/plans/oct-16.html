Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/16/26
Central Focus:

Students will read body language and move like animals.

Lesson # 11 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will act out emotions in charades for the group to guess.
  • Students will move like the animals on the movement cards.
  • Students will freeze as statues of a called feeling.
Resources and Materials:

Feeling word cards, movement cards

Learning Objectives:

I can read feelings on faces and bodies. I can move like the animals.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Social Awareness):

Children read the feelings of others from faces and body language, and respond with care.

TimeActivity:
6 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and Have You Filled a Bucket Today? (revisit).
Teacher: Teases the game: “Today we read feelings with NO words — just bodies!”
Students: Show one feeling with only their face.
5 min Mini-lesson: Reading body language.
Teacher: Models: whispers an emotion to themselves, acts it, then asks “How could you tell I was happy? My smile! My bouncy walk!”
Students: Point to the clues — the smile, the walk — on the teacher.
12 min Activity: Feelings Charades + movement cards.
Teacher: Whispers an emotion to one child at a time; then holds up movement cards.
Students: Charades: act WITHOUT words while the group guesses. Movement cards: Wiggle like Om Octopus · Wings up like Two Plus Toucan · Stomp like Bubbles.
4 min Game: Emotion statues.
Teacher: Calls a feeling — “angry!”
Students: Freeze as that feeling’s statue. Wobbliest statue is out for one round — gently!
3 min Closure: “Show me with your BODY (no words!): excited.”
Students: Freeze as excited; hold it until the teacher counts to three.
Assessment:
  • Observation during charades: can the child name the acted feeling and say how they knew (smile, walk)?
  • Exit check: freeze as EXCITED with your body — no words.
