Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/06/26
Central Focus:

Students will tell wants from needs and hear and make rhymes.

Lesson # 4 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will sort picture cards into wants and needs on a T-chart.
  • Students will decide whether word pairs rhyme.
  • Students will clap rhymes and cross arms for non-rhymes.
Resources and Materials:

Wants/needs picture cards, chart paper, markers

Learning Objectives:

I can tell a want from a need. I can hear when words rhyme.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Awareness):

Children begin to understand their own needs, make simple choices, and explain their thinking.

TimeActivity:
7 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and My Magical Choices — Becky Cummings (begin).
Teacher: Frames the day: “Today we make choices — and play with words that sound the same!”
Students: Give one example of a choice they made today.
6 min Mini-lesson: Wants vs. needs + what a rhyme is.
Teacher: Models: “It’s freezing outside — do we NEED a coat, or just WANT it? Do we NEED ice cream?” Then: “Rhyming words sound the same at the end — like cat and HAT!”
Students: Thumbs-up: does NEED a coat? Does ice cream rhyme with…?
14 min Activity: Wants vs. Needs T-chart + rhyme practice.
Teacher: Draws the chart; holds up picture cards one by one.
Students: Argue it out — that’s the learning! Then: hear word pairs (no/go, up/pup/cup); clap the rhymes, cross arms for non-rhymes.
4 min Game: Rhyme or not?
Teacher: Says word pairs quickly.
Students: Clap if they rhyme, cross arms if they don’t. Fastest correct wins the round.
4 min Closure: “Tell me one thing you NEED and one thing you WANT.”
Students: Each child answers before leaving the circle.
Assessment:
  • Observation during the T-chart: can the child sort a card and give a reason (“We need a coat because it’s cold”)?
  • Exit check: tell me one thing you NEED and one thing you WANT.
