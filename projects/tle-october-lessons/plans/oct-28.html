Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/28/26
Central Focus:

Students will paint fall trees, survey the class, and count the votes.

Lesson # 19 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will paint a fall tree with trunk and red, orange, and yellow leaves.
  • Students will vote in the weather survey and count which side has more.
  • Students will sort picture cards into FALL vs NOT FALL.
Resources and Materials:

Paint, paper, fall tree reference, sticker leaves, survey chart, season cards

Learning Objectives:

I can paint a fall tree. I can vote and count with the class.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Awareness):

Children observe changes in the world around them, share their ideas, and count together with the group.

TimeActivity:
7 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and Have You Filled a Bucket Today? (begin).
Teacher: Shows the fall tree reference: “Today we paint what WE see outside.”
Students: Name one fall thing they saw on the way to school.
6 min Mini-lesson: Tree painting + how to vote.
Teacher: Models: paint the trunk, then fingerprint leaves in red, orange, yellow. Models the survey: “Is it getting COLDER or WARMER outside? Place your leaf sticker to vote.”
Students: Practice one fingerprint leaf on scrap paper.
14 min Activity: Tree Painting + Weather Survey + Season sort.
Teacher: Hands out paint and paper; puts up the survey chart; lays out the season cards.
Students: Paint your fall tree (fingerprints make great leaves!) and dictate or write what is happening in your picture. Place a leaf sticker to vote: colder or warmer? Count together — which side has more? Sort picture cards: FALL vs NOT FALL (pumpkin? yes. snowman? not yet!).
4 min Game: Fall or not fall?
Teacher: Flashes a season card quickly.
Students: Shout “FALL!” or “NOT FALL!” First correct voice wins the round.
4 min Closure: “Which won — colder or warmer? By how many?”
Students: Announce the winner and the count together.
Assessment:
  • Observation during Season sort: does the child correctly sort fall vs not-fall cards?
  • Exit check: which won — colder or warmer? By how many?
