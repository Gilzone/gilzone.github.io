Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/21/26
Central Focus:

Students will understand charity and build the class donation box.

Lesson # 14 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will learn this month’s philanthropy focus: CHARITY.
  • Students will decorate the class donation box with drawings of kindness.
  • Students will act out a giving story with Grace & Charity paper-bag puppets.
Resources and Materials:

Box, crayons, paper bags, art supplies

Learning Objectives:

I can explain that charity means giving to help. I can help build our charity box.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Social Awareness):

Children show caring for others through giving, and understand that even small acts — even a smile — count as charity.

TimeActivity:
8 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and the Philanthropy reader (begin).
Teacher: Opens with the focus: “This month’s focus: CHARITY. Charity means giving to help others — even a smile counts.”
Students: Show a charity smile to a partner.
7 min Mini-lesson: What giving looks like.
Teacher: Models with the puppets: “Puppet feels sad… what could Charity do?” Acts out the children’s first idea.
Students: Shout one idea for what Charity could do.
15 min Activity: Decorate the donation box + paper-bag puppet show.
Teacher: Covers a box and labels it with the class: OUR CHARITY BOX; hands out paper bags for Grace & Charity puppets.
Students: Every child adds one drawing of kindness to the box. Make Grace & Charity puppets; act out a giving story — children suggest, puppets act it.
5 min Game: What could Charity do?
Teacher: Gives a quick sad scenario — “A friend drops their crayons…”
Students: Shout what Charity would do. Kindest idea wins the round.
5 min Closure: “What could we put in our charity box this month?”
Students: Each child suggests one item; the class votes on the first three.
Assessment:
  • Observation during the puppet show: does the child suggest a giving action when the puppet feels sad?
  • Exit check: name one thing we could put in our charity box this month.
