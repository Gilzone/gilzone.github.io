Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/07/26
Central Focus:

Students will practice kindness (bucket filling) and build AB and AABB patterns.

Lesson # 5 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will hear how a full bucket means a happy heart.
  • Students will draw or dictate one kind action for the class bucket.
  • Students will build AB and AABB apple patterns.
Resources and Materials:

Paper, crayons, red/green apple pattern cards, paper bucket

Learning Objectives:

I can do something kind to fill a bucket. I can build an AB pattern.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Social Awareness):

Children show caring for others, notice when someone needs help, and practice kind actions toward classmates.

TimeActivity:
7 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and Have You Filled a Bucket Today? — Carol McCloud (begin).
Teacher: Opens the story: “A full bucket means a happy heart. Let’s fill buckets today!”
Students: Show a full-bucket face and an empty-bucket face.
6 min Mini-lesson: How to fill a bucket.
Teacher: Models: “When I share my crayons, I fill a bucket. Watch.” Shares with a child and narrates.
Students: Tell the teacher one kind thing they could do today.
14 min Activity: Fill-a-bucket draw + apple patterns.
Teacher: Hands out paper and crayons; tapes finished drawings around the paper bucket on the wall.
Students: Draw or dictate one kind action you did or will do. Then build apple patterns: red, green, red, green… “What comes next?” Build AB, then AABB (red, red, green, green). Take your strip home.
4 min Game: What comes next?
Teacher: Starts a pattern with apple cards — red, green, red…
Students: Shout the next card. First correct voice places it.
4 min Closure: “Whose bucket did YOU fill today?”
Students: Each child answers; the class fills one more bucket by cheering.
Assessment:
  • Observation during patterns: can the child extend an AB pattern, then try AABB?
  • Exit check: name one kind action you did or will do today.
