Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/27/26
Central Focus:

Students will check in on their feelings and meet letters O–T.

Lesson # 18 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will point to the check-in poster and name how they feel.
  • Students will learn letters O–T, each with a motion and sound.
  • Students will hunt for hidden letter cards by their sounds.
Resources and Materials:

Feelings check-in poster, letter cards O–T

Learning Objectives:

I can check in on my feelings. I know letters O to T and their sounds.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Awareness):

Children name and describe their own emotions, and ask for connection — or space — in healthy ways.

TimeActivity:
6 min Introduction: Emotions Check circle and Listening to My Body (begin).
Teacher: Points to the check-in poster: “How are you feeling today?” Offers high-fives: “High-five if you want one — no thanks is okay too!”
Students: Each child points and names their feeling.
5 min Mini-lesson: Letters O–T.
Teacher: Holds up each card with its motion + sound: Ollie Ostrich · Pedro Porcupine · Quincy Quail · Ronnie Rhino · Sandy Snake · Tiny T-Rex · Umpy Umbrellabird.
Students: Copy every motion and sound.
12 min Activity: Letter hunt.
Teacher: Hides the letter cards around the room.
Students: “Find the letter that says /s/!” Hunt, find, and hold it up with its sound.
4 min Game: Sound dash.
Teacher: Calls a sound — “/t/ /t/…”
Students: Race to touch the matching hidden card. First touch wins the round.
3 min Closure: “How are you feeling RIGHT NOW? Point and tell me.”
Students: Each child points to the poster and names it before lining up.
Assessment:
  • Observation during the hunt: does the child find the letter that matches the called sound?
  • Exit check: point to the poster and tell me how you feel right now.
