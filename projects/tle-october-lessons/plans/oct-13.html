Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/13/26
Central Focus:

Students will connect colors to feelings and hear the first sound in their names.

Lesson # 8 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will splat-paint the color of their feeling today and name it.
  • Students will walk the gallery and notice how many feelings fit in one classroom.
  • Students will hear and repeat the first sound in their names.
Resources and Materials:

Paint, paper, name cards

Learning Objectives:

I can paint the color of my feeling. I can hear the first sound in my name.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Awareness):

Children name and describe their own emotions and begin to notice what makes them unique, including their own names.

TimeActivity:
7 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and The Color Monster (student-led retell).
Teacher: Hands the retelling to the children: “You know this book now — you tell it!”
Students: Retell the feelings and their colors in their own words.
6 min Mini-lesson: Colors hold feelings; names hold sounds.
Teacher: Models: “I’m feeling yellow-happy today!” Then: “Maya, I hear /m/ at the beginning of MAYA!”
Students: Point to the color of their own feeling; repeat the /m/ sound.
14 min Activity: Emotion Color Splat + gallery walk + name sounds.
Teacher: Hands out paint and paper; tapes finished paintings up for the walk.
Students: “What color is your feeling today?” Splat-paint that color and name the feeling: “I’m feeling yellow-happy!” Walk the gallery together: “Look how many feelings fit in one classroom.” Then go around the circle: each child hears and repeats the first sound in their name.
4 min Game: Whose sound?
Teacher: Says a first sound — “/m/…”
Students: Shout whose name starts with that sound. First correct voice wins the round.
4 min Closure: “What sound does YOUR name start with? Let’s all say it.”
Students: Each child says it; the whole class echoes.
Assessment:
  • Observation during splat-paint: does the child name a feeling and pick a matching color for it?
  • Exit check: say the first sound in your name.
