Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/22/26
Central Focus:

Students will observe what’s inside a pumpkin and complete fall puzzles.

Lesson # 15 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will name the pumpkin’s parts: seeds, pulp, skin, stem.
  • Students will count seeds by tens on counting strips.
  • Students will cut out and rebuild 4-piece pumpkin puzzles.
Resources and Materials:

Pumpkin (teacher cuts beforehand), sandwich bags, seed counting strips, puzzle copies, child-safe scissors, glue

Learning Objectives:

I can name the parts of a pumpkin. I can count seeds by tens.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Awareness):

Children use their senses to explore the world, describe textures, and share what they notice.

TimeActivity:
8 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and Breathe Like a Bear (begin).
Teacher: Teases the day: “Today we open something big and orange… what could be INSIDE?”
Students: Guess — seeds! slime! — then settle in for the story.
7 min Mini-lesson: Pumpkin parts + the safety rule.
Teacher: TEACHER ONLY cuts. Models the parts: “Seeds! Pulp! Skin! What’s the stem for?” Scoops pulp into a sealed bag for safe squishing.
Students: Echo each part name and point to it on the pumpkin.
15 min Activity: Pumpkin parts + seed counting + fall puzzles.
Teacher: Hands out the sealed pulp bags; then the counting strips and puzzle copies.
Students: Squish the pulp safely in the bag. Glue 10 seeds on a counting strip — “How many strips make 20?” Cut out the 4-piece pumpkin puzzles, mix, rebuild. Race a friend — then help them finish.
5 min Game: Name the part.
Teacher: Points to a part of the pumpkin — stem, skin…
Students: Shout it: “Seeds! Pulp! Skin! Stem!” First correct voice wins the round.
5 min Closure: “What did the inside of the pumpkin feel like? Slimy? Bumpy?”
Students: Each child answers with one feeling word.
Assessment:
  • Observation during puzzles: can the child rebuild the 4-piece pumpkin puzzle, and help a friend finish?
  • Exit check: describe the inside of the pumpkin in one word — slimy? bumpy?
