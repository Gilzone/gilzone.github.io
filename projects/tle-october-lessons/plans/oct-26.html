Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/26/26
Central Focus:

Students will discover color mixing (red + yellow = orange) and explore textures.

Lesson # 17 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will discover how to make orange from red and yellow paint.
  • Students will paint paper pumpkins with their mixed orange.
  • Students will describe textures at the Texture Station rotations.
Resources and Materials:

Red + yellow paint only, paper pumpkins, texture trays

Learning Objectives:

I can mix red and yellow to make orange. I can describe textures I feel.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Awareness):

Children use their senses to explore materials, describe what they feel, and share discoveries with the group.

TimeActivity:
8 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and The Color Monster (begin).
Teacher: Notes the mood: “Halloween week begins — excitement will be HIGH. Extra leaf-breathing breaks.”
Students: Show one leaf breath to start calm.
7 min Mini-lesson: The orange mystery.
Teacher: Sets out NO orange paint — only red and yellow: “Hmm, pumpkins are ORANGE… what can we do?!”
Students: Guess wildly; watch the teacher’s first mix.
15 min Activity: Pumpkin Paint discovery + texture stations.
Teacher: Hands out the red and yellow; gasps along with the class at the first orange. Then opens the Texture Station rotations: bubble wrap · playdough · slime · finger paint.
Students: Mix your own orange and paint a paper pumpkin; write or dictate one word about fall. Rotate through textures: “Describe what you feel: squishy? bumpy? smooth?”
5 min Game: What do you feel?
Teacher: Passes one mystery texture item around the circle.
Students: Feel it and describe it in one word — squishy! bumpy! smooth!
5 min Closure: “What color did YOU make today? How?”
Students: Each child answers: “Orange! Red and yellow!”
Assessment:
  • Observation during painting: did the child mix red and yellow, and can they name the color they made?
  • Exit check: what color did you make today, and how?
