Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/02/26
Central Focus:

Students will practice calm breathing they can use all month.

Lesson # 2 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will do breathing exercises together with the read-aloud.
  • Students will practice leaf breathing: trace the leaf, breathe in going up, out coming down.
  • Students will play the Go, Go, Go! circle game.
Resources and Materials:

Leaf breathing cards, music

Learning Objectives:

I can take a slow leaf breath when I need to calm down.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Management):

Children use calming strategies, with adult support, to manage strong feelings and return to a calm state.

TimeActivity:
6 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and Breathe Like a Bear — Kira Wille (begin).
Teacher: Starts the story and does the first breath WITH the children — they copy what they see, not what they are told.
Students: Copy every breath the teacher does.
5 min Mini-lesson: Leaf breathing.
Teacher: Models with a leaf card: “Trace the leaf slowly with your finger. Breathe IN while you go up… breathe OUT while you come down.”
Students: Practice once along with the teacher.
12 min Activity: Read + breathe together, then leaf breathing ×3.
Teacher: Reads Breathe Like a Bear, pausing for every breath; then hands out leaf cards.
Students: Do every breath in the book. Then trace your own leaf three slow times: in going up, out coming down.
4 min Game: Go, Go, Go! circle game.
Teacher: Starts the song.
Students: Stand in a circle; one child dances in the middle while everyone sings, then picks the next dancer.
3 min Closure: “When could you use leaf breathing?” (Before nap, when angry, when too excited.)
Students: Answer, then do one slow leaf breath together before lining up.
Assessment:
  • Observation during the activity: does the child trace the leaf and breathe in going up, out coming down, in rhythm?
  • Exit check at line-up: each child shows one slow leaf breath.
