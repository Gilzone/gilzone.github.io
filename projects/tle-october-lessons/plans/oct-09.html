Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/09/26
Central Focus:

Students will use their bodies to calm down and sing the fall-colors song.

Lesson # 7 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will rotate through four self-regulation stations with a drum beat.
  • Students will sing Red, Orange, Yellow, Brown while touching colors on the leaf poster.
  • Students will freeze-dance, freezing in a calm pose when the music stops.
Resources and Materials:

Self-regulation cards, leaf color poster, music

Learning Objectives:

I can use my body to calm down. I can sing the fall colors song.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Management):

Children use their bodies and calming strategies, with adult support, to manage strong feelings and return to a calm state.

TimeActivity:
6 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and Breathe Like a Bear (revisit).
Teacher: Reminds the class: “We learned leaf breathing — today our whole BODY helps us calm down.”
Students: Show one leaf breath from yesterday.
5 min Mini-lesson: The four calm-down moves.
Teacher: Models one station: “Stomp it out like Bubbles the Elephant!”
Students: Copy the stomp; guess the other three stations from the cards.
12 min Activity: Self-regulation circuit + fall-colors song.
Teacher: Runs the drum beat; rotates the class through four stations, one minute each: Stomp it out like Bubbles the Elephant · Stretch tall like TechniCal · Balance like Flexi Flamingo · Roll your shoulders like Ping Panda.
Students: Rotate with the drum beat. Then sing Red, Orange, Yellow, Brown to Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes, touching the colors on the leaf poster.
4 min Game: Freeze dance.
Teacher: Plays the music; stops it suddenly.
Students: Dance while it plays; when it stops, freeze in a calm pose — “Freeze like a statue feeling CALM.”
3 min Closure: “Which calm-down move is YOUR favorite? Show me.”
Students: Each child shows their move; the class copies it once.
Assessment:
  • Observation during the circuit: does the child rotate with the drum beat and try each station’s move?
  • Exit check: show me your favorite calm-down move.
