Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/15/26
Central Focus:

Students will express what brings happiness and blend compound words.

Lesson # 10 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will draw and dictate what makes them happy for the class chart.
  • Students will clap word parts together into compound words.
  • Students will act out compound words for the group to guess.
Resources and Materials:

Chart paper, markers, word cards

Learning Objectives:

I can say what makes me happy. I can clap two words into one.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Self-Awareness):

Children name and describe their own emotions and what causes them, and express their ideas through drawing and words.

TimeActivity:
7 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and My Magical Choices (revisit).
Teacher: Starts the chart: “I am happy when…” and draws their own answer.
Students: Tell the teacher one thing that makes them happy.
6 min Mini-lesson: Saying happy + blending words.
Teacher: Models: “I am happy when I see my son smile.” Then slowly: “sun… flower” — claps it together: “SUNFLOWER!”
Students: Clap along and shout the blended word.
14 min Activity: I Am Happy When… chart + compound word blend.
Teacher: Writes each child’s dictated sentence on the chart; then calls out word parts: cup+cake, pine+cone, scare+crow, rain+bow.
Students: Draw your answer and dictate the sentence. Then clap the parts together: “SUNFLOWER!”
4 min Game: Act it out.
Teacher: Pulls a compound word from a hat and whispers it to one child.
Students: The child acts it WITHOUT words; the group guesses.
4 min Closure: “Finish this: I am happy when…” (everyone answers!)
Students: Each child answers; the class cheers every answer.
Assessment:
  • Observation during the blend: does the child clap “sun… flower” together into “SUNFLOWER”?
  • Exit check: finish the sentence — “I am happy when…”
