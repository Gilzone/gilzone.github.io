Lesson Plan

Grade: UPK 4
Content Area: W.R.A.P. After-School · Social-Emotional Learning
Date: 10/29/26
Central Focus:

Students will meet letters U–Z plus long vowels and dance the leaves down.

Lesson # 20 / 21
Lesson Focus and Goals:
  • Students will learn letters U–Z (plus Amy Ape’s long A), each with a motion and sound.
  • Students will sing and dance The Leaves Are Falling Down with silk leaves.
  • Students will march in the letter parade with their favorite character.
Resources and Materials:

Letter cards U–Z, silk leaves

Learning Objectives:

I know letters U to Z. I can march in our letter parade.

Early Learning Standard (SEL — Relationship Skills):

Children celebrate learning together, take turns, and cheer for classmates in a shared parade.

TimeActivity:
7 min Introduction: Feelings check-in and My Magical Choices (begin).
Teacher: Builds the hype: “Today we finish the WHOLE alphabet’s characters!”
Students: Shout one letter they already know.
6 min Mini-lesson: Letters U–Z + long vowels.
Teacher: Holds up each card with its motion + sound: Uni Unicorn · Vanessa Vulture · Willy Wildebeest · Max Lynx (/x/!) · Yolanda Yak · Zoe Zebra · Amy Ape (long A!).
Students: Copy every motion and sound; celebrate: “We know the WHOLE alphabet’s characters!”
14 min Activity: The Leaves Are Falling Down + letter parade.
Teacher: Hands out silk leaves; starts the song to Farmer in the Dell.
Students: Sing with the silk leaves — twirl, drop, swirl, stomp them. Repeat until giggles. Then the letter parade: each child picks a favorite character card and marches: “I’m Zoe Zebra, /z/!”
4 min Game: March and say.
Teacher: Calls a letter — “Z!”
Students: March in place and shout the character + sound. Loudest correct march wins the round.
4 min Closure: “Which character was YOUR favorite this month?”
Students: Each child answers with the character and its sound.
Assessment:
  • Observation during the parade: does the child say the correct letter, character, and sound when marching?
  • Exit check: which character was your favorite this month — and what sound does it make?
