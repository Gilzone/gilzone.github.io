🎨 TLE After-School Lessons

October 2026 · UPK 4 W.R.A.P · Monthly theme: “My Emotions and Feelings”

Afternoon extends the morning’s learning through play. One read-aloud + two activities a day.

⬇ Download the spreadsheet
📅

Oct 1

Thu

📖 Read-aloud: The Color Monster — Anna Llenas

1. SEL: Emotion cards — identify & match facial expressions (Sad / Happy / Excited with Bubbles the Elephant)

2. Art: Colorful Jars — paint a jar for each emotion, like the Color Monster

Materials: Emotion cards, clear plastic jars, paint (yellow, blue, black, red, green)

💡 Theme launch. Set up Circle Time Box. Sign of the month: 'Dad' (open hand, thumb taps forehead).

📅

Oct 2

Fri

📖 Read-aloud: Breathe Like a Bear — Kira Wille

1. SEL: Leaf Breathing — trace the autumn leaf with a finger; breathe in at 'breathe in', out at 'breathe out'

2. Music & Movement: 'Go, Go, Go!' circle game — each child takes a turn in the middle

Materials: Leaf breathing cards, music

📅

Oct 5

Mon

📖 Read-aloud: Listening to My Body — Gabi Garcia

1. Science: Five-senses nature walk — collect leaves, sticks, acorns; what do you see / hear / feel?

2. Math: Leaf Sort — sort collected leaves by color and size (red, orange, yellow, brown)

Materials: Magnifying glasses, collection bags, sorting trays

📅

Oct 6

Tue

📖 Read-aloud: My Magical Choices — Becky Cummings

1. SEL: Wants vs. Needs T-chart — sort picture cards; 'Do we need a coat when it is cold, or just want it?'

2. Literacy: Rhyme intro — 'What is a rhyme?' + Clap the Rhyme (no/go, up/pup/cup)

Materials: Wants/needs picture cards, chart paper, markers

📅

Oct 7

Wed

📖 Read-aloud: Have You Filled a Bucket Today? — Carol McCloud

1. SEL: Filling a Bucket — draw or write about kind actions that fill buckets

2. Math: Apple Patterns — match and build AB and AABB patterns with red/green apple pictures

Materials: Paper, crayons, red/green apple pattern cards

📅

Oct 8

Thu

📖 Read-aloud: The Color Monster (revisit — student retell)

1. Science: Sink or Float — predict, test, and chart pumpkins, gourds, pinecones, rocks

2. Art: My Pumpkin — write your name on your pumpkin, decorate with paintbrushes

Materials: Sensory table + water, natural items, small pumpkins per child, paint

📅

Fri

📖 Read-aloud: Breathe Like a Bear (revisit)

1. SEL: Self-regulation cards — Stomp like Bubbles the Elephant, Stretch tall like TechniCal, Balance like Flexi Flamingo, Roll like Ping Panda

2. Music & Movement: 'Red, Orange, Yellow, Brown' (to the tune of Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes)

Materials: Self-regulation cards
