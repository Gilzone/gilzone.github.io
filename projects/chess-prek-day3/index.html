CHESS FOR PRE-K

Day 3: Gobble Up! 🍽

When your piece lands on an enemy, it gobbles it up. Yum!

📢 Teacher: tap your white piece, then tap a red-ringed enemy to gobble it. Pieces gobble the same way they move.

Part 1 — Watch them gobble

Choose your piece, tap it, then tap a red enemy.

Part 2 — Game: gobble them all!

Gobble all 3 enemies to win the round.
